Rainbrew Sky
Privacy Notice and Game Rules

Version 1 · 27 October 2026 · Rainbrew Coffee Shop (“Rainbrew”, “we”, “us”) runs Rainbrew Sky.

In short: to play Ranked and join leaderboards we keep your name, your mobile number and your game scores. We use them only to run the game, check scores are fair, show the leaderboards and reach contest winners. Other players never see your mobile number. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you for advertising. You can ask us to see, fix or delete your data at any time.

Privacy Notice

This notice follows the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) of the Philippines. By ticking the box when you register, you agree to it.

1. Who is responsible

Rainbrew Coffee Shop is the “personal information controller” for Rainbrew Sky. See Contact us below.

2. What we collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone, payment details or date of birth. The game has no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies. It has no purchases with real money: gems are earned in play.

Some things stay only on your own phone (your settings, your unicorns and gems, your game history, which players have used the phone). They are not sent to us.

3. Why we use it (purposes)

  • To run Rainbrew Sky: scores, leaderboards, circles and events.
  • To check that scores are real (we replay the flight on our server) and to remove cheating.
  • To run Rainbrew contests: find winners, contact them, give prizes and keep a record of prizes given.
  • To keep the game safe: remove names and circles that are offensive, and stop abuse.

We will not use your data for anything unrelated. If we ever want to use it for something new, we will ask you first.

4. Who can see your data

  • Everyone who can open a leaderboard: your name, score and the unicorn you used.
  • Members of a circle you join: your name and score in that circle.
  • Rainbrew staff: your name, mobile number and score records, only as needed for contests, fair play and support. Staff need a private PIN.
  • Our service providers that run the game for us: GitHub (hosts the game pages) and Google (Google Apps Script and Google Sheets, where scores are stored). They process data only to provide those services, and their servers may be outside the Philippines.
  • Authorities, only if the law requires it.

We do not sell or rent your data.

5. How we protect it

The game and the server use secure connections (HTTPS). Scores are signed and checked by the server. Staff access needs a private PIN, and the mobile numbers are visible only on the staff page. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach could seriously affect you, we will tell you and the National Privacy Commission as the law requires.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data only as long as needed for the purposes above. Scores and contest and prize records are kept while a season or contest runs and afterwards for record keeping; records of players who have not played for about 12 months are deleted or anonymised. If you ask us to delete your data sooner, we will (see section 8).

7. Children

Rainbrew Sky is for everyone, but if you are under 18 you need a parent or guardian to read this notice and agree before you register. The registration screen asks you to say so. A parent or guardian may ask to see or delete a child’s data at any time and is the one who claims a prize for a child. There is no chat in the game, and nobody can message you through it. Never put your phone number, address, e-mail or social media names in your name or in a circle name; the game blocks this.

8. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to:

  • be informed (this notice);
  • see what data we hold about you (access);
  • correct it if it is wrong (rectification);
  • object to its use, withdraw your agreement, and ask us to delete or block it (erasure);
  • receive a copy of it (data portability);
  • claim compensation if you were harmed by misuse of your data; and
  • complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

To use any of these rights, contact us (below) and tell us your name and mobile number as registered. We may ask you to prove it is you. We reply as soon as we can, normally within 30 days. If you ask us to delete your data, we remove your name and mobile number from every score, take your scores off the leaderboards, remove you from circles, and hide circles you own. Please note that deleting your data means you can no longer play Ranked under that name, and we may keep a record of a prize already given if the law or a dispute requires it.

9. Contact us

Email: rainbrew.coffeeshop@gmail.com (please write “Rainbrew Sky privacy” in the subject).
You can also ask any Rainbrew Coffee Shop staff member at our counter, or message us on Facebook (RainbrewCoffeeShop) or Instagram (@rainbrew.cs).

Questions and requests about your data are handled by the person in charge of privacy at Rainbrew Coffee Shop: Mary Clemente, HR Officer.

10. Changes to this notice

If we change this notice in a way that matters, the game asks you to read and agree again. The version number at the top tells you which one you agreed to.

Game Rules and Terms

11. Playing and leaderboards

  • Ranked is the leaderboard mode. In a season everyone plays the same course, and your best verified score counts. Classic is casual.
  • Playing Rainbrew Sky is free. No purchase is necessary to play or to win.
  • You must register with your own name and your own Philippine mobile number (09…). Use a name that is fine for everyone to read.
  • Circles are private boards you share with a code. The person who creates a circle may remove members or close it. Treat other members with respect.

12. Fair play

  • Every Ranked score is replayed on our server. A score that fails the check is not recorded.
  • Do not use bots, auto-players, edited copies of the game, other people’s numbers, or any trick to get a score the game would not allow. We may remove scores, names and circles and disqualify a player who does.
  • Scores close to a perfect game, or that look unusual, are checked by staff before any prize is given.
  • We may remove or rename any name, circle name or event note that is offensive, that shows contact details, or that pretends to be someone else.

13. Contests and prizes

  • Rainbrew sometimes gives small prizes to players. These are occasional and are not promised for any particular day or week. For each contest, the prize is announced in the game when the contest starts. No purchase is necessary to play, to win or to claim a prize. A prize is never tied to buying anything.
  • When a prize is given, the top three players (1st, 2nd and 3rd place) on the verified Ranked leaderboard win a small prize each. The prize for each contest is shown in the game when that contest starts. Winners are chosen only by their highest verified Ranked score when the period closes. A tie goes to the player who reached the score first.
  • How you will be told: by a text message to the mobile number you registered, or by a notice that appears in the game after the contest ends (shown once to each winner), or both. We contact the registered number only about prizes and contests. Staff never ask for payment, a password or a PIN.
  • How to claim: show your 6-digit verification code and a valid ID at Rainbrew within the claim period given in the message. The prize is for the registered player and cannot be exchanged for cash or passed on. A player under 18 claims with a parent or guardian. A prize that is not claimed within that time lapses and is not given to anyone else.
  • Staff decisions about scores and prizes are final. We may disqualify anyone who breaks these rules, and we may change, pause or cancel a contest or the game if we need to.
  • Rainbrew Sky contests are not sponsored, endorsed or run by Facebook or Instagram, and you release them from any responsibility for them.

14. The game and our responsibility

The game is provided as it is, and it may sometimes be offline or lose a score. We work to avoid that. To the extent the law allows, Rainbrew is not responsible for losses from using the game. Nothing here limits your rights under Philippine law, including consumer and data privacy rights.
